Exercise with Intention

Rooted in the experience of our most talented Strength Coaches. This combination workout trains several muscle groups, with a base movement and a second movement whose repetitions increase in a pyramid style.

Book a Free Assessment

300 Routine

300 total reps. Base movement: 10 squats every round. Pyramid: push-ups go up by 2 each round.

  • Round 1: 10 squats + 2 push-ups
  • Round 2: 10 squats + 4 push-ups
  • Keep climbing until you reach 300 reps

2 Dumbbell Workout

All you need is a pair of dumbbells. 4 rounds, rest 60 seconds between rounds.

  • 12 goblet squats
  • 10 dumbbell rows per arm
  • 10 shoulder presses
  • 12 Romanian deadlifts

Experience Calisthenics

Bodyweight only. Do it at home or in the park. 3 rounds.

  • 8 pull-ups (or band-assisted)
  • 15 push-ups
  • 20 walking lunges
  • 30-second plank
